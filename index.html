About Swdh

Swdh is designed to connect your devices and make sharing messages and files simple.

Our goal is to make paired-device communication convenient without repeatedly exchanging contact details.

Help

  1. Register your device with a display name.
  2. Generate a pairing code on one device.
  3. Enter that code on the other device.
  4. After pairing, select the device to communicate.
  5. Use Messages or File Sharing to exchange data.

Pairing and sharing require the backend services to be configured and running.
